Problem statement
Students who are involved in doing online research spend hours trying to look for the most eligible websites that contain information they can cite from. However, as the internet is so vast, it's sometimes impossible to look for a website with authors who are working on the same issue (or contradicting it). That's why reasearch_ai comes in handy, its an AI agent that can recognize and look for sites that store worthy information that actually helps the user. Instead of finding websites that sort of contain ideas that the user needs, the AI specializes in only finding published writing from proper organizations.
